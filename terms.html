These terms govern your use of goldenvalleygunsllc.com and any purchase, order, build, or transfer request made through it or in person with Golden Valley Guns LLC ("we," "us," "our"), an FFL 07 licensed firearms manufacturer and dealer in Golden Valley, Arizona.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By using this site, submitting an order, or working with us on a build or transfer, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the site or place an order.

2. Who May Use This Site

You must be at least 18 years old to use this site, consistent with the age verification you confirmed on entry. Some products and services (NFA items, certain firearm transfers) have additional legal age or licensing requirements beyond 18, which we'll walk you through directly.

3. Firearms Sales & Transfers

All firearm transfers happen in person at our shop, regardless of how the order or request started online. By federal law, every firearm transfer requires an in-person NICS background check and ATF Form 4473 at pickup. Nothing involving a firearm ships to you. Paying online only reserves the item.

Restricted states: we do not sell, ship, transfer, or source firearms for customers in California, New York, New Jersey, Massachusetts, Connecticut, Illinois, Maryland, Hawaii, Rhode Island, or Washington D.C., due to state and local law.

4. Special Orders

Items we source specifically for you, from our catalog or a distributor rather than our own on-hand stock, are special orders. Once placed, a special order is final sale, since we're ordering it in specifically for you. The one exception: if your order includes a firearm and you're denied by the NICS background check at pickup, you'll receive a full refund.

Questions before you pay for a special order? Call or text us first at (928) 727-0893. It's easier to sort out before payment than after.

5. Returns & Refunds (General Merchandise)

For non-firearm parts and accessories purchased directly from our own in-stock inventory (not a special order, see Section 4), we handle returns on a case-by-case basis. Contact us before sending or bringing anything back so we can work out what's right for your situation.

Firearms are never returnable once transferred, consistent with standard FFL practice and ATF recordkeeping requirements.

6. Pricing & Availability

Prices and availability are subject to change without notice. Distributor catalog listings reflect what's typically available from our suppliers but aren't a guarantee of in-stock inventory until we've confirmed it. That's part of why those items go through the special order process in Section 4 rather than an instant cart checkout.
