NFA Builds

The ATF Transfer Process

NFA items require ATF approval before they leave our license. Here's how we handle it.

Step 01

Build & Function Check

We complete the build and run a full function check. You get a finished, fully functioning firearm before any paperwork is filed.

Step 02

ATF Form Filed

We submit the appropriate ATF form on our end. We'll let you know when it goes in and what form was used.

Step 03

ATF Review Period

ATF reviews and approves the transfer. eForms typically clear in 2 to 8 weeks. We'll reach out as soon as anything comes back.

Step 04

Approved & Ready

Once ATF approves, we contact you to arrange pickup or transfer to your FFL. You take possession through your dealer.

What you need to do during ATF review: Nothing. We manage the paperwork and the wait. We'll call you when approval comes through. Questions about your specific build? (928) 727-0893.
