Questions

Frequently Asked

Common questions about builds, transfers, and how we operate.

Do I Need to Call Before Stopping By?

Yes. Give us a call before you head over so we can make sure we're available. We're a working shop, not a walk-in retail store.

Do You Handle FFL Transfers?

Yes, in and out of state. Call before you ship anything our way so we can confirm the details and get set up on our end.

Do You Work on NFA Items?

Yes. We hold an FFL 07 license, so NFA items and Class III transfers are handled. Give us a call if you have questions.

How Long Do Builds Take?

Depends on complexity and parts availability. We give you a straight timeline upfront and let you know if anything changes.

Do You Do Parts Builds?

Yes. AK kits, AR builds, and other parts-based projects are a big part of what we do. Reach out and we'll talk through what you're looking to build.

Do You Buy Used Firearms?

Yes. Bring it in and we'll give you a real number. We buy, sell, and trade firearms and gear at fair prices.

Can I Ship a Firearm to You?

Yes, for FFL transfers. Call us before you ship so we can confirm all the details on our end first.

What Areas Do You Serve?

We're in Golden Valley, AZ, serving Golden Valley, Kingman, and the surrounding Mohave County area.
