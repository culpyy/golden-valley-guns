What We Do

Our Services

Custom builds, parts work, gunsmithing, and transfers. All out of Golden Valley, AZ.

01

Custom Builds

Built to your specs from the ground up. We specialize in custom parts builds and will function check everything before it leaves the shop. If it doesn't run right, it doesn't go out.

02

Gunsmithing

Trigger work, parts fitting, repairs, and general gunsmithing. Bring us the problem and we'll give you a straight answer on what it needs and what it'll cost.

03

Parts Building

We build and source parts for custom projects. Whether you're looking to upgrade, repair, or build from scratch, we can help source and fit the right components for your firearm.

04

FFL Transfers

We accept in-state and out-of-state FFL transfers. Give us a call before you ship anything our way so we can make sure everything is ready on our end.

05

Buy, Sell & Trade

We buy, sell, and trade firearms and gear. Fair prices and honest assessments. Bring what you have and we'll give you a real number.

06

Class III / NFA

We hold an FFL 07 license. NFA items and Class III transfers are available. Give us a call if you have questions about any NFA work.
