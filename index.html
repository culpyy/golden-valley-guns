Questions

Frequently Asked

Common questions about builds, transfers, and how we operate.

Do I Need to Call Before Stopping By?

Yes. Give us a call before you head over so we can make sure we're available. We're a working shop, not a walk-in retail store.

Do You Handle FFL Transfers?

Yes, in and out of state. Call us first, then print our intake form and include it when you ship it our way.

Do You Work on NFA Items?

Yes. We hold an FFL 07 license, so we handle NFA items and Class III transfers. Give us a call if you have questions.

How Long Do Builds Take?

Depends on complexity and parts availability. We give you a straight timeline upfront and let you know if anything changes.

Do You Buy Used Firearms?

Yes. Bring it in and we'll give you a real number. We buy, sell, and trade firearms and gear at fair prices.

What Does the Quoted Price Cover?

The quoted price is what you pay to get a completed, fully functioning firearm back. Quality checks happen at every step of the build. No hidden fees at the end.
